# 0010. A feel carries exactly two modes

- **Status:** ⛔ Superseded by [0040 — A feel carries two to four modes, and the sets may overlap](0040-a-feel-carries-two-to-four-modes.md)
- **Date:** 2026-08-29
- **Source:** [feature-3, epic 3 Q4](../../specs/features/feature-3/prd/)

## Context

Sixteen grooves across four feels had to cover eight modes. The mapping from
feel to mode could be decorrelated deliberately, or chosen for musical fit.

## Decision

Each template carries exactly two modes, and the six pairs are disjoint —
accepting that feel and mode become correlated, because an idiomatic groove is
worth more than a decorrelated one.

## Consequences

- The feel became a strong clue to the mode, which the player can learn.
- With one mode offered by one feel only, the dominance guard's floor sat where
  that feel's groove count sat — the problem quick-12 later had to raise.
- Withdrawn in feature-25: five new styles could not be fitted into disjoint
  pairs without inventing mode assignments nobody would play.
